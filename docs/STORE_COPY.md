# Store listing draft (English)

## Display name
Mix Queue Fix for YouTube™

## Short description
Restores the hidden upcoming-tracks queue on YouTube Mix pages with a simple on/off switch.

## Long description
When a YouTube Mix hides its upcoming tracks and shows regular recommendations instead, Mix Queue Fix brings the Mix queue back automatically.

Features:
- Restores the Mix playlist queue on desktop.
- Reapplies the fix as you navigate between videos.
- Lightweight popup with an ON/OFF switch.
- Saves your preference locally.
- No sign-in, analytics, advertisements, tracking, or data collection.

Works in Microsoft Edge; designed for Chromium browsers, including Chrome and Brave.

This independent extension is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by YouTube or Google. YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC.

## Permission explanation
`storage`: remembers whether the extension is enabled or disabled.

## Site access explanation
Access to `youtube.com`: needed solely to find the hidden Mix playlist element and restore its visibility when enabled.
