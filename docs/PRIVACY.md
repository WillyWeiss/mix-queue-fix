# Privacy Policy — Mix Queue Fix

**Last updated:** 31 October 2026

Mix Queue Fix runs locally in your Chromium-based browser and restores the visibility of the hidden upcoming-tracks queue on YouTube Mix pages.

It does **not** collect, sell, share or transmit personal data. It does not access your Google account information or analyse your viewing history. No analytics, advertisements, external services, remote scripts or tracking cookies are included.

The extension stores only one preference, `enabled`, in the browser's local extension storage to remember whether the fix is switched ON or OFF.

Its content script executes on YouTube pages to detect the Mix playlist queue and remove its `hidden` attribute when the feature is enabled. It does not send page content to a server.

The extension is an independent project and is not affiliated with YouTube, Google, Microsoft or Brave.

For privacy queries, contact the developer via the eventual GitHub repository's Issues feature. (Add the public repository URL once created.)
